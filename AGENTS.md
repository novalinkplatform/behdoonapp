# Workspace Guidelines & Quality Rules

## 1. Reality Checker & Evidence-Based Verification
- **Default Status**: Every implementation or deployment starts as `NEEDS WORK`.
- **Proof Required**: Every change must be verified against actual code, live HTTP responses, and multi-viewport rendering before reporting completion.
- **Security Standard**: High-security architecture. All passwords, session tokens, and sensitive credentials must be hashed, salted, and cryptographically verified.
